01 SHOW NOTES

In this episode, I sit down with Dr. Rob Clark (@bigbadvet) to talk about the entrepreneurial side of building a specialty practice where the work is high stakes, the clients are emotional, and reputation is everything.

We go beyond the clinical side and get into the real drivers of a great service business:

- How specialization becomes your marketing
- Why “simple, consistent” beats “complex, clever” in outcomes
- The client experience pieces that create loyalty and referrals
- Communicating clearly when people are stressed and unsure
- Building standards, systems, and consistency behind the scenes
- What it takes to earn trust at scale, one interaction at a time
