01 IN THIS EPISODE

Brock Murray, co-founder of Ottawa marketing agency seoplus+, explains how search is shifting from Google rankings to AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude, and why his 50-person agency launched a generative engine optimization (GEO) service to help clients show up in AI answers. He and David compare notes on running AI agents inside a company, the coming rise in compute costs, the SaaS-to-results-as-a-service shift, and how a founder retreat changed the way Brock manages energy and burnout. The conversation ends with Brock's advice for young entrepreneurs: adopt a growth mindset, step out of the comfort zone, and trust your gut.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Brock's agency grew a two-location phone-repair client into more than 150 locations through SEO, and that account became the template for its ideal customer: multi-location businesses with 5 to 400 sites.
  2. Generative engine optimization (GEO) uses the same principles as SEO, which is why seoplus+ launched it as a service; Brock says right now is the easiest it will ever be to rank in AI tools because competition and algorithm sophistication are both low.
  3. In AI tools you track prompts rather than keywords, and the metric is impression share: run a prompt 100 times and count how often your company appears, since every chat has its own memory and no two answers are identical.
  4. Brock's citation data: Reddit was the top source cited by ChatGPT a year ago, LinkedIn is second, and older review sites like Yelp and Foursquare have regained importance.
  5. Brock expects AI compute costs to rise sharply once subsidies end, so early adopters on grandfathered pricing (his team pays $100 a month for Salesforce licenses versus $300 for new customers) hold a lasting cost advantage; his agency's tool budget is roughly $400,000 a year.
  6. David got a client referral directly from Claude, which pointed a Toronto physiotherapist to Taxifi after the AI could not finish his taxes, a sign that AI tools are already sending business to companies they know about.
  7. After a three-day Limitless founder retreat run by former Shopify CFO Mark MacLeod, Brock now prioritizes sleep, breathing exercises and a slower morning, arguing that consistency beats intensity for a founder's energy.
