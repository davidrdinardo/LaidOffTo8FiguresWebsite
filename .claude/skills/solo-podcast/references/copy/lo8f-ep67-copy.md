Taxifi V2 Is Here: AI Does Your Books, a Human Checks Every File

**Description**

Taxifi version 2 is live. We started building on January 1st, 2026, and every feature since then has been tested by paying users before we added the next one. E-transfers need your input once. After that the app remembers the payee.
