Why I Fired My Marketing Agency and Became a Media Company

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 IN THIS EPISODE

David DiNardo explains why he stopped working with a marketing agency and brought his website, SEO, and content in-house while building his AI accounting firm, Taxifi. The agency delivered good work, but even a 48-hour turnaround was too slow, so he connected Claude Code to Netlify so that every prompt pushes live to his site and used Higgsfield to polish the graphics. He argues that with AI, speed and control are the real advantage, and that any small business owner can now operate as a media company and build proprietary systems that add to the value of the business.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. The problem with an agency is not quality but speed; because you are one of many clients, even a 48-hour turnaround is too long when AI can make the change in seconds.
  2. David's stack is Claude Code for building and editing the site, Netlify for hosting connected to his GoDaddy domain so every prompt deploys automatically, and Higgsfield for polishing graphics.
  3. Seeing changes in real time lets you decide whether a design is right, something you cannot judge from a brief you hand to an agency.
  4. Using this workflow, Taxifi's website went from six pages to 160 pages in a week, and a new Google review was added as a testimonial within seconds of receiving it.
  5. SEO and GEO can now be run in-house too; David connects Claude Code to Google Search Console and asks it what to change to rank on both Google and AI search.
  6. In AI you have to be first, fast, and furious; a polished six-to-nine-month, $100,000 agency website can be archaic by the time it ships because competitors move faster.
  7. Building your own apps and website internally turns them into proprietary assets that raise the value of your business if you ever sell it.
