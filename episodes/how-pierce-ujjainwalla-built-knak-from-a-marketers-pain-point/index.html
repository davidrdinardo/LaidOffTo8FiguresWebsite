How Pierce Ujjainwalla Built Knak from a Marketer’s Pain Point

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 IN THIS EPISODE

Pierce Ujjainwalla, co-founder and CEO of Ottawa-based Knak, an AI campaign-creation platform used by enterprise marketing teams, describes how a two-day company hackathon with Claude Code changed how his roughly 150-person team builds product. He walks David DiNardo through running Knak as a side project for almost five years, the toxic-culture stretch that showed up in eNPS scores, why taking money from Insight Partners was one of his best decisions, and where AI is pushing email and lifecycle marketing next.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Pierce's hackathon lesson: with Claude Code, product requirements, design and engineering happen simultaneously, so a year-long roadmap should compress by 5x to 10x and a month-long task should now be scoped as a week.
  2. Knak developers have shifted from writing code to reviewing it; Pierce is re-pointing sprint estimates because a 10-point story may now be a one-point story.
  3. Pierce built both his companies as side projects: he cut his salaried job to four days at 80% pay to start a consulting firm, and ran Knak for nearly five years on the side until three large customers closed in one month.
  4. Knak's early eNPS was around 100 (a good score is roughly 40); it fell as the company grew because Pierce hired the wrong leaders and then kept jumping back in, and fixing it meant changing people, working with an Enneagram coach and joining a TEC Canada peer group.
  5. Pierce uses the interview process from the book 'Who' by Geoff Smart and still does the final interview for senior hires; a six-person people-and-culture team with two recruiters supports a company nearing 150 staff.
  6. Knak sells mostly to US companies with $500 million or more in revenue, and most pipeline still comes from inbound demo requests on the website; outbound and partnerships are the channels being built now.
  7. Pierce's hill to die on: email will stay a critical channel because it is the only list you fully own, while open rate is the metric he trusts least since Apple and security bots inflate it.
03 SHOW NOTES

In this episode, I sit down with Pierce Ujjainwalla, co-founder and CEO of Knak and founder of Revenue Pulse. After building his career in the marketing trenches at IBM, SAP, NVIDIA, and Marketo, Pierce launched Knak in 2015 to make campaign creation easier for marketers. Today, Knak helps enterprise teams create emails and landing pages with no code, and the company has grown to serve hundreds of brands and tens of thousands of marketers.

We get into what it takes to turn real industry experience into a scalable software company, how to build in a crowded market, and why creativity still matters when you’re growing fast. If you’re a founder, operator, or marketer building something from the ground up, this episode is for you.
