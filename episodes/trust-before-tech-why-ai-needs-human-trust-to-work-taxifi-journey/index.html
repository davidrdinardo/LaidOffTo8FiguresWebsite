Trust Before Tech: Why AI Needs Human Trust to Work (Taxifi Journey)

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 IN THIS EPISODE

David DiNardo shares a hard lesson from building Taxifi: more than 20 people signed up on launch day and not one completed the process, because people do not yet trust an AI app with their financial data the way they trusted a handshake with their accountant. He explains why trust has to be built before technology, and how he vibe-coded an entire sales funnel with Claude over a weekend so he can test messaging on social media and Meta ads instead of waiting on agencies.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Over 20 people signed up for Taxifi on day one and none finished onboarding; David's diagnosis is that the trust gap between a person and an AI app is bigger than any efficiency gain.
  2. In a service business the relationship is person to person; with an AI product it is person to app to model, and the trust has to be rebuilt at each link before people will hand over bank and tax data.
  3. David's blind spot was optimizing for speed, efficiency and accuracy when customers actually worry about AI, security and data first.
  4. He built a complete sales funnel, landing pages and customer-journey mapping with Claude in six to eight hours over a weekend, and estimates a repeat would take two to four hours.
  5. Rather than refining in a back room with a marketing agency, put drafts in front of the market through social polls and Meta ads and let consumer reaction decide the messaging.
  6. Many AI companies are failing because they are tech-heavy and skip customer behaviour; mixing a human layer into the AI product may be what the market needs until it is ready.
  7. His two rules for founders documenting a build: nail the customer journey first, then iterate quickly using vibe-coding tools, and spend 90 percent of the time listening to feedback and 10 percent talking.
