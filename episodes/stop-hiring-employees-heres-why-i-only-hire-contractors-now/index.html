Stop Hiring Employees. Here's Why I Only Hire Contractors Now

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 SHOW NOTES

Employees or contractors? It used to be a payroll question. With AI models dropping monthly instead of every six months, it&#39;s now a speed question — and in this solo episode I lay out exactly how I&#39;m answering it at Taxifi: contractors only, paid a premium, chosen per project, so I can move as fast as the tools do.

I walk through the real trade-offs — Ontario labour rules vs. the freedom to end a contract tomorrow, the 75–100% premium contractors cost, SR&ED credits that favour employees, and why I&#39;m choosing speed anyway. Plus the two things that convinced me: a Claude Skills setup that erased four or five admin hours a month in an afternoon, and a "weeks-long" build my developer shipped in three to five hours on a Saturday. The old playbook is out. This is the honest restructuring conversation every founder needs to have now.
