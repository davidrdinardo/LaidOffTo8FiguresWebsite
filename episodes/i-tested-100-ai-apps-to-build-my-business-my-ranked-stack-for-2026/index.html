01 IN THIS EPISODE

David DiNardo ranks the AI apps he actually uses every day to build Taxifi, scoring each from 1 (best) to 4 (worst) after testing more than 100 tools over the past year. Claude, Manus and Perplexity Computer come out on top, ChatGPT gets a failing grade for acting like a yes-man, and he explains how Claude Design and Nano Banana replaced thousands of dollars in designer costs.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. David ranks ChatGPT a 4 out of 4 for business use because it acts as a yes-man; he now uses it under 15 minutes a day for personal questions like pool setup.
  2. Claude earns a 1: he prompts it to act like an Elon Musk-level executive that resolves objections before answering, to get execution-ready output instead of back and forth.
  3. Claude Design (ranked 2) replaced his Figma workflow: Taxifi's first design iteration cost $7,000 and months, while he built 10 iterations of V2 himself in a week, saving an estimated 3 to 6 months.
  4. Nano Banana (ranked 2) cut his thumbnail cost from $60 each to a flat $25 a month for all podcast, YouTube, Spotify and Instagram images.
  5. ElevenLabs (ranked 2) cloned his voice from podcast audio, cutting a 30-second reel's voice-over from two hours to under 30 minutes, though it still sounds a little robotic.
  6. Intercom's Fin gets a 3 only because he has not finished training it; Manus, an agent that audits Meta ads and websites while clicking through your screen, gets a 1.
  7. Perplexity Computer built a working prototype overnight that Claude Code could not, because it leverages more connectors and tools without manual installation.
