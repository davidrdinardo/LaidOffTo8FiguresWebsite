Nobody Wants a Tax App: 5 Lessons From Building Taxifi in Public.

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 SHOW NOTES

This week’s Taxifi build update came with a few lessons I didn’t expect, and they’re changing how I think about the product.

Here are the 5 biggest takeaways so far:

1) Nobody wants a tax app. They want the problem gone.
2) Niche makes people lean in. The clearer the “who,” the stronger the pull.
3) Judgment free is a real feature. People don’t just need software, they need a safe place to start. (So many people apologize for being behind.)
4) Simple beats clever. The clearest promise wins:
“One-click, judgment-free tax filing so you never think about taxes again.”
5) Building in public changes how you ship. More feedback, more accountability, and faster decisions.

If you’re building a product, a brand, or anything customer-facing, this episode is a reminder that clarity, safety, and simplicity are the real differentiators.
