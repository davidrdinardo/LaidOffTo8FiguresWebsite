Sergey Poltev: Your AI Is Only as Secure as Your Password

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 SHOW NOTES

Sergey Poltev got his first computer at 12, broke it, and was told by his mom to learn to fix it himself. He went on to study computer science and cybersecurity in Russia, then built his career on the other side: keeping businesses safe from the hackers he grew up watching. Today he runs a managed IT, AI and cybersecurity company serving 10-to-50-person businesses, the size he calls the "hacker sweet spot," and he wrote Hack Proof: A Guide to Cybersecurity for Your Business.

Three out of four cyberattacks are now AI-related, and most small business owners have no idea what tools their employees are pasting client data into. We get into his Simplify, Secure, Scale model, shadow AI, why your cyber insurance might not pay out, the $47 non-compliance fee that quietly voids your coverage, ransomware that locks your whole Outlook, the 200-day average before a breach is detected, and why "human in the loop" is the rule that keeps an AI agent from emptying your bank account.
