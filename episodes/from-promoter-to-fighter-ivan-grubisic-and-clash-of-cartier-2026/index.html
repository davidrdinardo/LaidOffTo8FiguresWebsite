From Promoter to Fighter: Ivan Grubisic and Clash of Cartier 2026

01 IN THIS EPISODE

Ivan Grubisic, a Canadian Armed Forces sniper turned recruiter who took up boxing at 38, and his coach Roberto join David DiNardo a week before Clash of Cartier, the Ottawa military boxing gala Ivan co-promotes and fights in. They break down how the volunteer coaching program turns soldiers into fighters, why sparring is a classroom rather than a contest, and how the event funds youth scholarships while showcasing the Ottawa military community.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Clash of Cartier started as a Governor General's Foot Guards 150th anniversary idea and is now in its fourth year, with 16 fighters on the card, roughly 450 to 500 attendees at the Rogers Centre, a pay-per-view stream around $20, and 28 applicants for four youth scholarship awards.
  2. Roberto's rule: you play soccer, but you cannot play boxing; the ring corrects emotion and ego immediately, which is why he treats sparring as a mirror and a classroom, not something you win.
  3. Coaches deliberately handicap stronger sparring partners, for example making Ivan switch from southpaw to orthodox or work only defense, so beginners learn safely and veterans are forced to train their weaknesses.
  4. Ivan started boxing at 38, dropped from over 200 lb to 179 lb largely through skipping rope, and credits 20 years of military discipline for being able to follow corner instructions with 500 people screaming.
  5. Roberto, a former teacher, argues the ability to sit through struggle and boredom has eroded since smartphones arrived around 2011-2012, and boxing forces a return to basics like human interaction and immediate feedback.
  6. The program is open to any active Canadian Armed Forces member who can attend weekly training in Ottawa from October through March; two coaches work each corner and the event is entirely volunteer and sponsor funded.
  7. David's marketing advice for the gala: document the selection process and training as short mindset clips on social media, which he argues would lift both attendance and military recruiting.
03 SHOW NOTES

Today’s episode is live with Ivan Grubisic a Clash of Cartier promoter and co-main event fighter as we go behind the scenes of the fourth annual Clash of Cartier, the sold-out charity boxing event taking place March 7, 2026 at Rogers Centre, Ottawa.

We talk about the work it takes to build a night like this, the mindset behind stepping into the ring yourself, and why this event means more than boxing, with proceeds supporting the Governor General’s Foot Guards Regimental Association Youth Awards Program for youth connected to Canada’s military community.
