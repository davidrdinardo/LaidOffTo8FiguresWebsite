How to Sell Any Service: My Exact Sales Process in Under 10 Minutes

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 IN THIS EPISODE

David DiNardo lays out the two-call sales process he used to close nearly every client at his accounting firms and now runs at Taxifi: a discovery call where he listens 80% of the time to find the real pain point, then a proposal call within seven days where he presents bronze, silver and gold pricing. He explains why you never quote on the first call, how to space the three tiers so the middle package wins, the fast-action offer that gets the signature, and the $99 bronze-tier mistake he made with Taxifi's own pricing.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. The discovery call is 80% listening and 20% talking; talk more than you listen on call one and you lose the client, because they still don't know you.
  2. Never give a price on the discovery call. Ask for the information you need to build a proposal, then book the proposal call within seven days while the problem is still fresh.
  3. Remove every friction point in getting that information: email, phone, text, a shared Dropbox, whatever is easiest for the client.
  4. The proposal call flips the ratio to 80% you talking, runs under 20 minutes, and replays the pain points you heard on call one before you show a price.
  5. Always present three prices. In David's example bronze is $200, silver $250 to $300, gold $1,500 to $2,000; fewer than 10% pick bronze and almost nobody picks gold, so build 80% of your operations around the middle package.
  6. Close with a fast-action offer, such as three months free if they sign before the end of the week, so there is urgency on the proposal.
  7. David's own mistake at Taxifi: bronze at $99 against silver at $299 was too wide a gap and pushed people to the bare-bones plan, so he raised bronze to $199 to steer clients toward silver.
