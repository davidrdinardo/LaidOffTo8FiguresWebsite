Result as a Service: How I’m Building Taxifi to Be AI Native

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 IN THIS EPISODE

David DiNardo gives an October update on building Taxifi and the thinking behind every decision: the shift to monthly bookkeeping clients, an AI chat that answers any question about a client's books in real time, and why he now frames the product as result-as-a-service rather than software-as-a-service. He explains how building AI native across the whole company, not just the product, keeps speed and control in-house, and previews the self-serve version two launching in early November.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Start documenting your process publicly; David says sharing the raw build of Taxifi has done more to propel the business than anything he did quietly with his previous companies.
  2. Taxifi moved from annual sole-proprietor work to monthly recurring bookkeeping, because real-time books are where clients actually feel the value.
  3. One client went from the red to the green in three months simply by having full visibility of her numbers through the AI chat; knowing your numbers lets you cut roughly 10% of expenses without harming the business.
  4. Think result-as-a-service, not software-as-a-service: every feature has to tie to a result for the client, like a filed tax return, or it doesn't get built.
  5. Pick the lane the big AI players won't touch; a vertical like filing someone's taxes is safer ground than mass-produced horizontal tasks.
  6. Marketing is the moat: David spends one to two hours every morning building content, and keeps SEO and the website in-house so a change is a prompt away.
  7. Build AI native across the whole business, using the 10/80/10 model (10% human upfront, 80% AI doing the work, 10% human review), and the self-serve Taxifi v2 ships at the beginning of November.
