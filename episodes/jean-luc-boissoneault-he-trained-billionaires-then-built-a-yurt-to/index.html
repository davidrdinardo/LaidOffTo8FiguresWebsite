01 SHOW NOTES

He placed 7th in the world in bodybuilding at 21, trained some of the richest people in Canada — including the CEO of Mattamy Homes — sold his fitness company, spent five years "doing nothing," and built a yurt in his backyard to meditate three hours a day. His neighbour thought he was crazy. His clients call him the most dangerous question-asker they know.

Jean-Luc Boissoneault coaches entrepreneurs on what he calls personal sovereignty — the five freedoms of health, time, money, purpose, and relationships. In this conversation: why 99% of people never share their genius, why you can&#39;t change your thoughts with more thoughts, the three-heart-attack story that will stay with you, and what an $8 billion founder does differently than everyone else.
