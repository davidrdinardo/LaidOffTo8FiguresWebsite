01 SHOW NOTES

Most owners say, “One day I’ll sell my business.”
Very few are building something anyone would actually buy.

In this episode, I sit down with Markian Pergat – entrepreneur, SMB investor and “business value & exit maker” – to talk about how to turn your business from a stressful job into a real, sellable asset.

We get into:
- The difference between a profitable business and an exitable one
- Why most owners regret their exit (and how to avoid it)
- How to make your business less dependent on you
- The first moves to start building an exit option, even if you’re not ready to sell

If you’re a founder, creator, or small business owner who wants freedom, options, and a business that can outlive you, this episode is a must‑listen.
