Taxifi V2 Is Here: AI Does Your Books, a Human Checks Every File

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →

01 IN THIS EPISODE

David DiNardo walks through version 2 of Taxifi, the AI bookkeeping and tax platform he is building for Canadian small businesses. He shows the full flow on screen: signing up, connecting a bank, seeing profit and cash flow in real time, how transactions get categorized, asking the app for reports, and bringing in a human when the AI is not sure. He closes with what is planned for version 3.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS

  1. Bookkeeping is a compliance requirement, so the goal is to make it fast, easy and cost-effective rather than something an owner has to think about.
  2. When the AI is fully confident, a transaction is categorized automatically. Anything less lands on a to-do list with a suggested category for the owner to confirm.
  3. E-transfers need the owner's input once. After that the app remembers the payee and categorizes it on its own.
  4. Owners can ask Taxifi plain questions, such as last month's income and expenses, and get the answer with a PDF.
  5. AI on a platform only goes so far. Humans still do the reconciliations and filings, and a person verifies every file before it goes out.
  6. Owners who do not want to connect a bank can upload or scan statements instead.
  7. Version 3 is planned to read uploaded documents, such as amortization schedules, and split principal from interest automatically.

03 SHOW NOTES

Taxifi version 2 is live. We started building on January 1st, 2026, and every feature since then has been tested by paying users before we added the next one. Every small business owner I know has the same problem. They don't know where their books are at, and they just want to run their business. So I took everything I've learned doing this and put it in one app.

In this episode I walk through the whole thing on one of my own rental properties: connecting the bank through Plaid, seeing profit and cash flow in real time, how the AI categorizes transactions and what lands on your to-do list, asking Taxifi for a report, and bringing in a human when the AI doesn't know. AI on a platform can only take you so far, so a human verifies every file before it goes out the door. I also show document uploads and what's coming in version 3.
