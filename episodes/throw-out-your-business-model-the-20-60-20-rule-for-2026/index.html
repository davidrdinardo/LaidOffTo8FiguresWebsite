Throw Out Your Business Model: The 20/60/20 Rule for 2026

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 IN THIS EPISODE

David DiNardo argues that the business models that worked in 2020 no longer apply, and lays out the 20/60/20 structure he is using to build Taxifi: 20 percent humans at the front of the process, 60 percent AI in the middle, and 20 percent humans closing the loop. He pulls together lessons from recent podcast guests on pricing at the extremes, validating demand with Meta ads before building, and why investors now expect growth in one year rather than five. The through-line is that the same effort, paired with technology, can move a business from a one-times-revenue valuation to five or ten times.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Rebuild your business model around 20/60/20: humans handle the first 20 percent of the process (sales and client touch), AI does the middle 60 percent, and humans close the final 20 percent (for Taxifi, verifying and submitting to CRA).
  2. Price either for the mass market or for very high-ticket clients; pricing in the middle does not work anymore.
  3. Before spending a dollar on technology, run Meta ads at about $20 a day for 30 days with a landing page and wait list, and see if you can book $40,000 to $50,000 of clients.
  4. Use Claude or ChatGPT to vibe-code a prototype of your process, then sell that prototype before you build the real thing.
  5. Investors in 2020 accepted $5 million in monthly recurring revenue in five years; now they want it in one year because the gap of disruption is so large.
  6. Nobody knows the true multiple of an AI-powered accounting or consulting firm anymore, because the market is moving too fast to price it.
  7. A traditional accounting firm sells for about one times revenue and a cloud firm for about two times, but the same firm with AI infrastructure could reach five, seven, or ten times revenue on the same effort.
