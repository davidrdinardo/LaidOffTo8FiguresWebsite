A Flood Destroyed Her Fashion Business. She Rebuilt in People's Closets

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 SHOW NOTES

A flood wiped out her clothing brand — a year of stock, her samples, and patterns she&#39;d spent years perfecting. Then her customers started asking a question that became a whole new business.

Rebecca Rowe trained at Pratt Institute in New York and studied in Paris, built a ready-to-wear clothing brand for women with athletic body shapes, and lost it all in an Ottawa flood. In this episode of Laid Off to 8 Figures, we get into how she rebuilt as a personal stylist, the "anchor point" method that changed how her clients see themselves, why fashion school teaches you to design but never to sell, and the insurance lesson every product business owner needs to hear before it&#39;s too late.
