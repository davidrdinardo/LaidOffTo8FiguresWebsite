01 IN THIS EPISODE

David DiNardo sits down with Ottawa-based marketer Martin DeSouza, who spent ten years building other people's personal brands before starting his own account and pulling 1.7 million views on a single reel within his first month. Martin lays out the mechanics behind that growth: social media has become interest media, hooks and formats matter more than what you say, and the goal for a founder is to build an audience brick by brick that attracts opportunity rather than fame. The conversation also covers his 30-day build-in-public series for ReadyCut, the top, middle, and bottom of funnel content mix, and how David should position Taxifi under his personal brand.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Start a fresh account rather than reviving an old personal one, because the algorithm has profiled your old followers and will stagnate posts they are not interested in.
  2. Instagram tests every post on buckets of roughly 200 people and only pushes it further based on watch time, saves, and shares, so shares are now the metric that matters most.
  3. Every viral video stacks several hooks at once: a text hook, a visual hook, an audio hook, an action hook, and a verbal hook in the first line, and the packaging matters more than the content that follows.
  4. Use YouTube as the largest validated content database: search your topic, take the top video titles as your topics, the thumbnail copy as your text hook, and the first line as your verbal hook.
  5. Commit to five years and treat it as a body of work; Mr. Beast will only consult with people who have already posted 100 videos because by then they no longer need him.
  6. Split content into top of funnel (maximum reach, follow as the CTA), middle of funnel (who you are, newsletter or community sign-ups), and bottom of funnel (direct sell), keeping the sell to under 10 percent of posts.
  7. Do not build a separate social account for Taxifi; run all content through David's personal brand and point the bio to a website or Stan Store where people can learn about the product and book a call.
