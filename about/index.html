ABOUT THE HOST

David DiNardo

I was laid off from the Ottawa Hospital at 24, a few weeks after finishing my MBA. That October I incorporated Envolta, a cloud accounting firm. Ten years later it had 45 people serving businesses across Canada, and in October 2022 I sold it — ten years to the month, which was the plan from day one.

THE SHORT VERSION
  1. 24Laid off from the Ottawa Hospital with a fresh MBA and no plan. I promised myself nobody would have more control over my trajectory than I did.
  2. 2012Incorporated Envolta in October. A cloud accounting firm when most firms still ran on paper and shoeboxes.
  3. 2019Seven years in, I finally matched my old hospital salary. Then it tripled, then quadrupled. Entrepreneurship is a long grind before the hockey stick.
  4. 2022Sold Envolta in October, 45 people and clients across Canada, to one of several buyers. Built as a sellable asset from the start.
  5. 2025Launched Laid Off To 8 Figures to have the conversations founders don't have on stage.
  6. 2026Building Taxifi, an AI accounting firm for Canadian creators and small businesses, and documenting it in public on the show.
WHY THIS SHOW

Every founder story you hear is told from the stage, after the exit, with the messy middle edited out. I spent ten years in that middle. Hard work didn't guarantee anything, a bigger salary didn't mean more take-home pay, and the thing that finally worked was a decade of uninterrupted reps while I had no mortgage and no kids to protect.

So this show does the opposite of the highlight reel. I sit down with real founders — mostly Canadian, a lot of them from Ottawa — and we go through the actual decisions: the layoff, the first dollar, the first hire, the month it nearly fell apart, the numbers behind the sale. In between, I record solo episodes about what I'm learning building Taxifi, in real time, mistakes included.

If you're in the messy middle right now, this is the show that tells you the truth.
