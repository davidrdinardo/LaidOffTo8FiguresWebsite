01 IN THIS EPISODE

David DiNardo explains how he replaced his video editor with Claude Code, Descript and Higgsfield: the raw recording goes in, and fifteen minutes later the full edit, three Shorts, the thumbnail and captions come out, for about $300 a month in apps he already paid for instead of $1,000 to $1,500 a month. He argues that technical work like editing is the first to go, and that the people doing it have two ways out: become the premium one percent, or package the system and sell it to business owners who will never set it up themselves.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. The setup is three apps David already paid for, Descript, Claude Code and Higgsfield, about $300 a month, replacing an editor who cost $150 an episode and $1,000 to $1,500 a month.
  2. Fifteen minutes after sending the raw recording to Claude Code he had the full edit, the reels and Shorts in his format, the thumbnail and the captions, plus an automation to post them.
  3. He gave Claude Code one of his editor's finished videos as the example to copy, which is how the format carried over.
  4. If you are an editor, sell the system instead of the hours: set up the automations for business owners and charge $199 a month; David says he would pay it and still save 80%.
  5. A hundred clients at $199 a month is a business; the technical work gets replaced, the formula and the distribution are what you sell.
  6. The middle ground is gone: be the one percent of the one percent that business owners pay $2,000 to $3,000 a reel for, or sell to the masses, but generalists will lose job after job.
  7. The same shift is coming for graphic design, bookkeeping and accounting, every knowledge-work niche, and the first movers who build a system inside a niche will make a very good living.
