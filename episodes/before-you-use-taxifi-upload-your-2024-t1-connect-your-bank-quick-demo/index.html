Before You Use Taxifi: Upload Your 2024 T1 + Connect Your Bank (Quick Demo)

01 IN THIS EPISODE

Two weeks before Taxifi's March 1 launch, David DiNardo demos the feature that lets self-employed users upload their 2024 T1 so OCR fills in their profile and business details automatically. He explains the four onboarding steps for a sole proprietor, why users should get their prior return and bank connection ready before signing in, and the founders-rate waitlist discount.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Taxifi onboarding for a sole proprietor has four steps in year one: complete your profile, set up your business, connect your bank for income and expenses, and authorize the CRA.
  2. Uploading your 2024 tax return lets OCR pull the profile and business fields automatically, so you do not answer questions or fill in forms; you just verify what the system found.
  3. Before you sign in, get your prior-year return from your accountant or download it from Wealthsimple or TurboTax so you are not searching mid-session.
  4. With last year's return and a bank connection in hand, David says the whole tax process should take under 15 minutes, likely under 10.
  5. Bookkeeping is done by a human training the AI model, with the goal of fully automating bank-statement categorization so users never print 12 months of statements for an accountant again.
  6. Joining the waitlist at taxifi.ai gives $100 off, a 50% discount, and locks in the founders rate for the first year.
03 SHOW NOTES

There are 2 things you need to do before you can get the full Taxifi experience:

1) Have your 2024 tax return (T1) ready so you can upload it and pre-fill your 2025 return
2) Connect your bank so AI can categorize your income and expenses automatically

In today’s episode, I walk you through the exact process of uploading your 2024 T1, what gets pulled in, how the pre-fill works, and I share a short demo so you can see it in action.

If you are a Canadian creator or solopreneur and you want taxes to feel simpler, this episode shows you how Taxifi is built to get you organized fast.
