01 IN THIS EPISODE

Nish Patel, an AI strategist and studio founder with no formal education past high school, argues that AI is "middleware for the human imagination" and that the real bottleneck is people, not the technology. He and David DiNardo work through why scale, speed and optimization are becoming commoditized, why a 100-true-customer business built on trust may beat a 100,000-customer platform, and how fresh grads, employees and business owners should each adapt before the "AI blast radius" reaches them.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Nish's framing: AI is probabilistic, not deterministic like CRMs and SaaS tools, so mastering the software matters less than unlocking your own curiosity, creativity and imagination.
  2. The AI "meteor" hit on November 30, 2022; like the dinosaurs, most organizations will not die instantly but will be consumed as the blast radius expands to all machine-like work.
  3. Scale, speed and continuous optimization are the natural terrain of machines and their cost trends toward zero, so durable value shifts to what cannot scale: deep human relationships and trust.
  4. Nish is building his AI studio around roughly 100 true customers rather than thousands, drawing on Kevin Kelly's 1,000 true fans, Seth Godin's sell-more-to-the-same-people and Dunbar's 150-person trust limit.
  5. After the SaaS sell-off following Claude Code's release, Nish predicts "too big to fail" gets replaced by "too big to survive" as small teams peel off niche customers from platforms like HubSpot.
  6. For employees: use your employer's AI training on their dime and, if laid off, treat severance as startup funding ("fired into funding"); for owners: if you are not learning AI, the blast radius is coming for you.
  7. Nish's sticky note: "Follow your inner algorithm" - know yourself, be yourself and show up as yourself, because a unique perspective is what AI cannot copy and commoditize.
