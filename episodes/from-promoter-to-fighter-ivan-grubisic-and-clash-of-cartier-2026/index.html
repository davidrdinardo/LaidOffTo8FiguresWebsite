01 SHOW NOTES

Today’s episode is live with Ivan Grubisic a Clash of Cartier promoter and co-main event fighter as we go behind the scenes of the fourth annual Clash of Cartier, the sold-out charity boxing event taking place March 7, 2026 at Rogers Centre, Ottawa.

We talk about the work it takes to build a night like this, the mindset behind stepping into the ring yourself, and why this event means more than boxing, with proceeds supporting the Governor General’s Foot Guards Regimental Association Youth Awards Program for youth connected to Canada’s military community.
