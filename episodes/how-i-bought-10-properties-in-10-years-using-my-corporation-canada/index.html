How I Bought 10 Properties in 10 Years Using My Corporation (Canada)

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 SHOW NOTES

At 24, I was let go from my corporate job. By 34, I was financially free — and along the way I bought 10 rental properties, one a year for 10 years. This is the exact playbook.

In this solo episode, I break down the corporate structure that made it possible: why I bought every property through a corporation instead of personally (12.2% small business tax vs. up to 54% personal), the one-holding-company-per-property system, why cash flow is non-negotiable on every purchase, and the monthly bookkeeping habit that once caught a $400 water leak my tenant never reported.
