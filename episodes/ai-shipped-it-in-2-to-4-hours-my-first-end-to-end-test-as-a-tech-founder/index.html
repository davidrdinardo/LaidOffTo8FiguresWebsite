AI Shipped It in 2 to 4 Hours: My First End-to-End Test as a Tech Founder: Building Taxifi in Public

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 IN THIS EPISODE

David DiNardo gives a build-in-public update on Taxifi 55 days before its March 1, 2026 launch: the first end-to-end test passed on January 5, a day ahead of the deadline his development team set over the holidays, and the 15-plus changes his team requested after reviewing the app were turned around in two to four hours. He lays out what the speed of AI-driven development means for business owners with teams resisting AI, why becoming a tech founder after 15 years running an accounting firm still requires technical fluency, and why building a culture of execution around deadlines matters more than hustle-culture debates.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Over 15 changes from the team's first review of Taxifi were fixed by the developers in two to four hours, a cycle that David says would have taken weeks or months of requirement documents and coding a few years ago.
  2. Business owners with a team of 30 that isn't adopting AI will be left behind; David advises identifying who is on board, having hard conversations with those who aren't, and doubling down on the people who are.
  3. After selling his accounting firm in 2022 and 2024 to get into tech, David says a non-technical founder still has to learn enough about front-end, back-end and AI to know who to talk to and how to build trust with the people executing.
  4. His third lesson is that you're either all in or all out: balance and boundaries matter, but a founder has to be obsessed with the business for it to grow.
  5. The development team set a holiday-period deadline of January 6 for the end-to-end test and hit it on January 5, with David paying for a subscription and getting his taxes done through the app as the proof.
  6. Build a culture of execution with an accountability chart, tough conversations and recognition when deadlines are hit, and keep writing V2 requirements while the team tests V1.
03 SHOW NOTES

Today’s episode is a real build in public update from inside Taxifi.

I ran our first true end-to-end test, found a bunch of changes that needed to happen, and then watched something wild: with AI in the workflow, those updates happened in real time. I’d prompt, the changes would happen, and within 2 to 4 hours we were in a totally different place.

This episode is also a personal milestone for me. After 15 years owning an accounting firm and the last year building my personal brand, I can finally say it: I’m officially a tech founder.

Here’s what I cover:

- The end-to-end test and what broke (and what we fixed fast)
- How AI is changing speed of development, prompt to production
- The mindset shift that comes with building in public
- Why you either go all in or you get left behind
- Accountability, deadlines, and what it takes to actually ship on time

If you’re building anything, product, brand, or business, this episode is a reminder that speed, obsession, and accountability win.
