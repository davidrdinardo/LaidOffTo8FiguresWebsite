01 IN THIS EPISODE

David DiNardo walks coach and strategist Michaela through a live, start-to-finish demo of Taxifi on its March 1, 2026 launch day, filing a sole proprietor's 2025 Canadian return in about 15 to 20 minutes. Uploading last year's return prefills the personal, rental, home-office and vehicle sections, a Plaid bank connection feeds an AI model that categorizes a year of transactions, CRA authorization pulls the slips, and a CPA prepares and e-files the return after a Stripe payment and a T183 signature. David explains why off-the-shelf tools like QuickBooks Online and Xero overwhelm solopreneurs and lays out the goal of a one-click, fully automated return in year two.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Taxifi's version one covers 2025 returns for sole proprietors in every province except Quebec, includes the spouse's return for free, and can also prepare and submit the GST/HST return.
  2. Uploading the full 2024 tax return takes under 30 seconds and prefills personal deductions, rental income and expenses, business details and the sales tax number as a reference point for 2025.
  3. Home office and vehicle expenses are not prefilled because people move; the user enters total square footage and workspace size, and Taxifi calculates the business-use percentage.
  4. Bank transactions come in through Plaid, and an AI model trained over the previous three months categorizes them as business, personal or meals, flagging only the handful it cannot resolve for the user to categorize or annotate.
  5. In Ontario, David notes, you must register for a sales tax number once revenue passes $30,000, and an annual filer's 2025 remittance is due March 31, 2026.
  6. After paying through Stripe, a tax professional prepares the return, the client signs the T183 online, and Taxifi e-files; a 24/7 chat in the app routes questions to the tax team.
  7. David's year-two vision is a phone notification saying your taxes are done: with onboarding and CRA authorization complete, the AI reuses prior data so no manual steps remain.
