01 SHOW NOTES

Alex Lawson grew up in Heron Gate with a single mom, the snowsuit fund and the food bank. Today he owns Capital Framers, runs a crew of 17, and is one of 14 candidates for Mayor of Ottawa. His pitch is on the sign: builder, not politician.

We get into why he voted for the current mayor and what changed, the "leaking buckets" problem at OC Transpo, what downtown business owners are actually telling him, the 82-year-old widow hit with the vacant unit tax, and what it took to stop being the bottleneck in his own company. Plus: what happens when Reddit finds out you&#39;re running.
