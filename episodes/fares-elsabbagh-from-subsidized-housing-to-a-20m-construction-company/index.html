Fares Elsabbagh: From Subsidized Housing to a $20M Construction Company

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 SHOW NOTES

He grew up in subsidized housing, hated being told what to do at McDonald&#39;s, and figured out at 15 he was "screwed for life" — so he built an eight-figure construction company without ever lifting a hammer.

Fares Elsabbagh is the founder of Ottawa General Contractors, a design-build company doing $20M+ across Ottawa, Halifax, and Vancouver. In this episode of Laid Off to 8 Figures, we get into how a Tim Ferriss book and a landing page started it all, the $1M walk-away offer he almost took right before COVID doubled his business, why he rebuilt the company around "construction experiences" instead of construction services, growing through acquisition, and the Ottawa zoning changes that turn almost any lot into a multi-unit opportunity.
