01 IN THIS EPISODE

David DiNardo previews Taxifi one month before launch and explains the three things it does on day one: connect to your bank and auto-categorize bookkeeping, OCR your previous tax return so 2025 needs no data entry, and file your taxes automatically. He walks through why side-hustle income pushes employed creators into a 40% marginal bracket, and asks solopreneurs to join the wait list as testers for a $100 founders discount.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Taxifi's day-one offer: bank-connected auto-categorization, upload of your prior return to skip data entry, and automatic tax filing, aimed at creators and solopreneurs.
  2. A $20,000 side business stacked on a $100,000 T4 salary is taxed on $120,000, so the marginal rate on that side income can be 40% rather than the 15% most people assume.
  3. Taxifi is training an AI model with creator-specific rules to assign expenses with a probability score (a Staples purchase is 100% supplies) and flag what you can legitimately claim.
  4. David's pricing target is about $200 for a solopreneur return, roughly five times cheaper than the thousands a CPA charges; incorporated and complex cases still get a human.
  5. The launch plan: February for testers with real data, March 1 for the wider public; wait-list sign-ups lock in $100 off as a founders rate, with extra incentives for selected early adopters.
