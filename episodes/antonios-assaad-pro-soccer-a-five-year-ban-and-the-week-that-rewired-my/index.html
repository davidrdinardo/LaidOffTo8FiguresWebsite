01 IN THIS EPISODE

Antonios Assaad, a former professional soccer player and coach from Ottawa, tells David DiNardo how a five-year youth ban, a decade of failed contracts in Lebanon and Germany, and a coaching stint at Club América and in Dubai left him living a double life of drinking and ego. A call from an old teammate sent him to Rythmia, a medically licensed ayahuasca retreat in Costa Rica, where one week dismantled his limiting beliefs and led him to turn down a contract, join the company's sales team, and rebuild his life around structure, service, and sobriety. The conversation covers what the week actually looks like, how he handled family judgment, and why he believes energy and environment decide who you become.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Antonios was banned from youth soccer for five years at 14 for spitting on the ground, and he believes those lost years of tactical development are why his pro career never got past short, failed contracts.
  2. He turned down a contract from the owner of his Dubai club after one unexpected phone call, trusting what he calls the strongest intuitive calling of his life; a year and a half later he says that call was absolutely right.
  3. Rythmia runs a structured week: yoga and three education classes each day, ceremonies Monday through Thursday night, and a 13-week integration program afterward, because insight without integration means going back to old habits.
  4. He credits giving money away to friends and family who needed it, rather than hoarding out of fear, with more coming his way, and argues you are not successful if the people around you are struggling.
  5. Who you surround yourself with is the number one lever: keep old friends but love them from far away, and put yourself at tables with people who think bigger than you.
  6. His advice to Canadian entrepreneurs is to leave a comfortable environment, because if you can make it in an uncomfortable one you can make it anywhere, and to pick a partner who operates at the same frequency as you.
  7. The sticky note on his first laptop would say: focus on the energy and the vibration, because that manifests in everything you do.
