I'm 38. Here's Exactly How I Became Successful in 10 Minutes

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 IN THIS EPISODE

David DiNardo, at 38, lays out the handful of things that shaped his path from a hospital IT job to founding and selling a 45-person accounting firm. He credits the people he surrounded himself with, a formal education that taught him how to learn, and a corporate job that exposed the limits of salaried work before a layoff pushed him into entrepreneurship. The core message is that the decade from 22 to 32, spent all in on business with no competing commitments, is what set up the financial freedom he has now.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Surround yourself with entrepreneurs and property owners; you become the people you spend time with.
  2. The most valuable thing education gives you is the ability to teach yourself anything on your own.
  3. Working for someone else showed David that hard work does not guarantee promotion and that a higher salary does not mean more take-home pay.
  4. Getting let go from the Ottawa Hospital was the best thing that happened to him because it led to founding Involta, which he grew to 45 employees across Canada and sold within 10 years as planned.
  5. If you are in your 20s with no family or mortgage, go all in on a business now, because those years of uninterrupted reps are what put the rest of your life in a strong financial position.
  6. Aim to trade your value for money rather than your time, through multiple income streams like business, investing, and rental properties.
  7. A mentor can only take you 20 to 30 percent of the way; the other 70 percent has to come from your own experience and mistakes.
