If I Lost Everything Today: My Exact First 90 Days

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 IN THIS EPISODE

David DiNardo walks through exactly what he would do in his first 90 days if he lost everything and had to start over. Rather than launching a tech company, he would use Claude or ChatGPT to shortlist boring local businesses, knock on doors to sell a service like mobile car cleaning before buying any equipment, then spend the next month servicing the first five customers himself to learn his true costs and margins. The final month is about repeating the script in a new neighborhood at a higher price, with the point being that selling before building is how you turn an idea into a viable business within six months.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Sell before you build: knock on doors and get people to commit to a service before spending a dollar on equipment, a website, or anything else.
  2. Prompt ChatGPT or Claude for the ten most boring businesses people need in your region, filter out the ones that need a license or degree you lack, and narrow to one to three that resonate with you.
  3. Start small and boring rather than with a tech firm, because you will make mistakes and it is better to make them in a small business first.
  4. If nobody buys after 100 pitches, do another 50, and only then move on to the next idea; you are spending time, not money, and gaining experience either way.
  5. Days 31 to 60 are for personally servicing your first five customers so you see how you get paid, what your supplies cost, and what you actually earn per hour.
  6. Do the math on cost of goods sold: if a $100 job costs $50 in product and takes three hours, you are making about $15 an hour, which tells you why the market charges $200.
  7. In days 61 to 90, take the sales script to a new neighborhood, sell 10 people instead of five at $125 instead of $100, and keep repeating that cycle for a year before changing course.
