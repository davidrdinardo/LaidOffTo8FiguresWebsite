**Title**

The Conference Trick No One Does (It Got Me the Speaker's Number)

**Description**

I use one trick at every conference and almost nobody else does it. Last week at SaaS North I picked a speaker whose company I wanted to partner with. I was the first one in the room and the last one out. When the Q&A opened I stood up, said my name and my company, and gave an honest testimonial about his product in front of about 50 people.

In this episode I break down what happened next: why he remembered me when ten people were lined up to talk to him, how I left with his number and an intro to his content manager, and why I am not pitching the partnership yet. Going to the conference is the top of the funnel, the conversation is the middle, and the partnership is the bottom. If you skip the first two, the pitch never works.

Takeaways:
Pick the speakers you want to partner with before the conference starts.
Be the first one in the room and the last one out, and speak up in the Q&A.
Give an honest testimonial in public before you ever ask for anything.
Do not pitch the partnership on day one. Build the relationship for six months to a year.
Partnerships have a funnel: showing up is the top, the conversation is the middle, the partnership is the bottom.

I do one thing at every conference that almost nobody else does.
