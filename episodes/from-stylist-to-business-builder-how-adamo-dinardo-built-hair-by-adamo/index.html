From Stylist to Business Builder: How Adamo DiNardo Built Hair by Adamo & Co.

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 IN THIS EPISODE

Ottawa hair stylist Adamo DiNardo, owner of Hair by Adamo & Co., walks David DiNardo through the business side of a career that started at age 14: opening a 700-square-foot salon on Preston Street after a phone call with his dad, packing 12 people into it within two and a half years, and signing a lease on a 3,000-square-foot space in the middle of the pandemic. He explains how he builds a culture of psychological safety with 17 staff, why he still works five days a week behind the chair, and how he is now carving out admin days to plan the next phase of growth.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Adamo started in salons at 14 and opened Hair by Adamo & Co. in a 700-square-foot space on Preston Street after telling his dad he wanted something small; a space was offered within eight hours.
  2. The first location grew to 12 people in 700 square feet in two and a half years before he knocked through to the unit next door and doubled the footprint.
  3. COVID shutdowns hit salons three times because they were not deemed essential; curbside product pickup, delivered by volunteering stylists, kept the business afloat.
  4. He signed the lease on a roughly 3,000-square-foot salon during the pandemic because capacity rules meant the old space could never bring back the same numbers.
  5. His core value is a safe space where staff and clients can be their authentic selves, and his rule for conflict is 'come to me first' before problems simmer and boil over.
  6. Adamo still cuts hair five days a week from 9 to 6 or 7, and says the hard part of business is fitting payroll, accounting and staff conversations around the craft he loves.
  7. In the past five years every one of his teammates has bought a home, which he counts as proof the business is working; his sticky-note advice is 'keep going, don't look back, and smile'.
03 SHOW NOTES

Adamo DiNardo is the stylist behind Hair by Adamo & Co, an Ottawa area salon focused on healthy hair and professional cutting, styling, and coloring.

In this episode, we go deep on the entrepreneurial journey behind building real success in a service business: craft, reputation, consistency, and the standards that turn first time clients into long term loyalty.

We talk about:

- The early days of getting clients and earning trust
- Why your client experience is your marketing (and why referrals compound)
- Building a brand in a crowded market without chasing trends
- Staying sharp through education and continuous improvement
- The mindset shift from being great at the craft to building a business around it.
