Co-Parenting Expenses Without the Conflict: Building Simple Split

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 IN THIS EPISODE

Nancy Gharib, founder of Simple Split, explains how her own divorce turned tracking kids' expenses across two households into a bootstrapped fintech app, eight years in the making and now backed by partners in Dubai and the US ahead of a planned April 2026 mobile launch. She walks through pitching Arlene Dickinson in a thousand-person Clubhouse room, growing up in her Lebanese parents' Ottawa grocery store, and how faith and identity shifts shaped the business. David and Nancy compare notes on building apps with Replit and Lovable, community-first products, and teaching teams to move from AI anxiety to AI confidence.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Simple Split started because divorce agreements don't make expense tracking easy: chasing receipts and money between two houses was a problem Nancy assumed she couldn't be the only one facing, and existing co-parenting apps were built for high-conflict custody, not finances.
  2. Arlene Dickinson told Nancy in a Clubhouse pitch room, 'don't change a thing about your pitch,' and said she wished she'd had the app during her own divorce, but told her to come back once she had customers.
  3. Validate before you build: Nancy's number-one advice is to poll Facebook groups and Reddit and talk to people in your network rather than guarding your idea, because the Ottawa entrepreneur community is extremely generous with feedback.
  4. The app is designed to work solo if the other parent refuses to participate, with a longer-term vision of shared 'piggy bank' accounts that teach kids compounding and financial autonomy from a young age.
  5. David suggests court-mandated adoption as a growth strategy, noting US courts already mandate co-parenting apps like OurFamilyWizard for high-conflict cases.
  6. Nancy self-funds Simple Split through management consulting and AI training, working on the app at the fringes of her day, weekends and holidays, with a goal of shifting to it full time.
  7. Both founders say AI prototyping tools like Replit, Lovable and ChatGPT cut 80 percent of the back-and-forth with developers, turning a three-to-four-month website build into a couple of weeks.
03 SHOW NOTES

Co-parenting finances can turn into receipts, screenshots, and text threads that create friction instead of clarity.

In this episode, I sit down with Nancy Gharib, Founder of Simple Split, a tool built to make expense sharing easier for co-parents (and even roommates) by creating a simple, transparent system for tracking and splitting shared expenses. One of the most interesting parts: the workflow is designed to be effortless, including a text-based experience where you can “flip a text” and the system handles the rest.

Nancy shares the story behind building Simple Split from lived experience, and what it takes to design a product around fairness, calm, and real-world behavior.

We get into:
- Why shared expenses break down (even when people mean well)
- Designing systems that reduce friction and emotional load
- Building something simple, transparent, and accessible
- Lessons from turning a personal pain point into a real product
