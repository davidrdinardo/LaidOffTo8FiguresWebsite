Distribution Is the Moat: 3 Things I Learned at SaaS North

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →

01 IN THIS EPISODE

David DiNardo shares the three ideas he brought home from two days at SaaS North, a conference of founders, creators and AI experts. The loudest message at a tech conference was that distribution is the moat, not technology: AI can build the app from a prompt, so the advantage goes to whoever has a personal brand and an audience that looks nothing like everyone else's. The other two were moving customers from monthly to annual recurring revenue so they stay long enough to build the product with you, and building the best agent for your industry, because whoever owns the agent will own the industry.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS

  1. Distribution is the moat, not technology. Five years ago it was technology first and distribution second; with AI building apps from a prompt, that order has flipped.
  2. Founders at SaaS North are going all in on personal brand because people buy from people, and content is what separates you once everyone can build the same product.
  3. One speaker showed five websites: four were AI-built and looked the same, and the fifth looked like nothing else. The one that is different is the one that gets distribution.
  4. Generic outreach and AI slop are already being ignored. Your brand and content have to be completely different from your competitors', because the norm won't sell.
  5. Get customers on annual recurring revenue instead of monthly, even if that means charging less and giving more, so they stay long enough to build the product with you instead of bouncing between platforms.
  6. Build the best agent for your industry: something that connects to the customer's Slack, Gmail and other tools, gathers the information and produces the output for them.
  7. The shift is from chat to agents, from asking a question to handing over work such as bookkeeping or a month of content, and whoever owns the best agent will own the industry.

03 SHOW NOTES

I spent two days at SaaS North last week with founders, creators and AI experts. For a tech conference, the loudest message in the room surprised me: distribution is the moat, not technology. Five years ago you built first and sold second. Now AI can build the app from a prompt, so the winners are the people with an audience and a personal brand that looks nothing like everyone else's.

In this episode I break down the three ideas I brought home: why distribution now comes before technology, why the smartest software companies are moving customers from monthly to annual and building the product with them, and why whoever builds the best agent for their industry is going to own it.
