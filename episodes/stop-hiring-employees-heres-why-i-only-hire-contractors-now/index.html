Stop Hiring Employees. Here's Why I Only Hire Contractors Now

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 IN THIS EPISODE

David DiNardo walks through the employee-versus-contractor decision every business owner now faces as AI models ship monthly instead of every six months. He weighs the trade-offs, from Ontario labour laws and SR&ED credits to contractor premiums and lack of commitment, and explains why Taxifi is hiring only contractors so it can move fast and swap people per project. The episode is a push to rethink team structure now rather than wait for the old playbook to stop working.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. In Ontario, labour laws make letting an employee go slow and rule-bound, while a contractor can be released immediately.
  2. Contractors can cost 75 to 100 percent more than an employee salary, may not treat you as their primary client, and can leave whenever they want.
  3. Employees carry advantages too: two weeks' notice, more commitment, and in Canada richer SR&ED credits for tech companies than contractors provide.
  4. David is hiring only contractors at Taxifi because he wants speed over cost, paying a premium to keep multiple contractors on the go and pick the best fit per project.
  5. If AI can produce the technical skills, hire for soft skills; roles like bookkeeper and account manager are being redefined.
  6. A few hours setting up Claude Skills reproduced David's video edits and posting, eliminating four to five admin hours a month.
  7. Rip out the old playbook and ask whether your staff can move at the speed of your business, because that is what will win or lose in this new world.
