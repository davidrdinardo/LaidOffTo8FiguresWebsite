Brian Claire: He Built a Payroll App Prototype in 10 Minutes

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 SHOW NOTES

Brian Claire got pushed into accounting by his mom, bounced through BlackBerry, the Ottawa Hospital and defence firms, then hung his shingle in 2018 and built Blueprint Accounting into a seven-person firm serving clients coast to coast. Along the way he kept running into the same problem: payroll tools built for one business at a time, not for an accounting firm running fifty of them.

So he summarized a Slack thread in Claude, pasted it into Cursor, and ten minutes later had a prototype. He showed it to accountant friends, surveyed 60 firms, found they were juggling five to six payroll platforms each, hired an agency to build it, launched to a room of 40 people with a live glitch, and signed his first paying client within six months. Then he merged with a developer who was building the same thing and rebranded to Nooma.

We get into why 300,000 accountants have left the industry, why the partnership model is dead, how a non-developer manages a dev agency, what to put in a shareholder agreement with co-founders, whether a payroll platform that replaces 80% of a person is still a $40-a-month product, and why Intuit might be coming for your clients.
