01 IN THIS EPISODE

David DiNardo talks with Gustavo Sanchez, co-founder of Booked, an AI front office for trades and home service businesses that answers calls within a second, qualifies leads, and books jobs while the owner is on site. Gustavo traces his path from arriving in Canada from Venezuela at 16 without English, through six years at Shopify during its rocket-ship growth, to raising a round in under a month, and explains why he believes AI lets software adapt to the user instead of the other way around. The conversation covers how to sell AI to a late-adopter industry, running a company on falsifiable hypotheses, hiring hungry early-career engineers, and why distribution now matters more than building, and ends with a live demo call to Booked's AI receptionist.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Roughly 30 to 40 percent of a trades business's revenue goes to voicemail; a roofer with a $15,000 average ticket who misses two calls a week is losing tens of thousands a month without knowing it.
  2. Respond to a new inquiry in under five minutes, because the caller who reaches voicemail simply dials the next business on the Google listing.
  3. To sell AI to late adopters, skip the AI pitch and make them do the math on their own missed calls, then earn trust in person at trade shows; Booked has attended about nine and talked to hundreds of owners.
  4. SaaS forced customers to learn a fixed workflow; with voice AI the interface is language, so the software can adapt to each user instead of an archetype.
  5. Run the company on falsifiable hypotheses and decide quickly to stop, hold, or double down; now that a feature takes a week instead of six months, focus and prioritization are harder, not easier.
  6. The cost of building has collapsed, so the premium is now on distribution and growth, and that is what will separate winners from losers.
  7. If you want a comfortable lifestyle business, bootstrapping is fine, but if you want to be a category leader in a fast-moving market, raise money for speed and hire high-trust, mission-driven people as fast as you can.
  8. Booked hires early-career people who fall in love with the problem and are resourceful, gives them full trust on day one, and brings engineers to trade shows and customer calls while Claude builds in the background.
