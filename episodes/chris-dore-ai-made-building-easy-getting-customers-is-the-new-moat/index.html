01 SHOW NOTES

Chris Dore is back — four months after his first episode, and the AI landscape has already flipped again. His thesis this round: the development piece is solved. Anyone can vibe code an MVP in a weekend. Which means the entire game has moved to the two things everyone skips — talking to customers before you build, and go-to-market after.

Chris runs Scarlet Nova (AI consultancy), teaches entrepreneurship at Algonquin College, and just launched Rogue Ventures, a venture studio building AI-native disruptors. We cover which LLM to actually pick (Gemini vs. ChatGPT vs. Claude), agents and swarms, the Applebee&#39;s AI that changed my mind, what he tells his students about careers AI will eat, and why every 20th Canadian being an entrepreneur isn&#39;t enough.
