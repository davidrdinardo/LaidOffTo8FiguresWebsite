AI Is Not The Strategy: How to Build Leverage in the AI Era

WATCH ON YOUTUBE → LISTEN ON SPOTIFY →
01 SHOW NOTES

AI is changing everything.

But the biggest mistake people are making is thinking AI is the strategy.

It’s not.

AI is the leverage behind the strategy.

In this episode of Laid Off To 8 Figures, I sit down with Nish to talk about AI, creativity, the future of work, and how founders, creators, and operators can actually use this shift instead of just watching it happen.

We get into how AI is changing the way we work, build, create, and compete, and why the people who win in this next era will not just be the ones using the tools. They will be the ones asking better questions, moving faster, and adapting sooner.

In this episode, we cover:

• Why AI is not the strategy
• How to use AI as leverage
• Why creativity still matters
• The future of work and business
• How founders and creators can adapt
• Why asking better questions is the real advantage

If you are building a business, growing a personal brand, or trying to understand where work is going, this episode is for you.
