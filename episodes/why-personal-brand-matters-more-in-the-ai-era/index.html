Why Personal Brand Matters More in the AI Era

01 IN THIS EPISODE

Kyle Turk, founder of Meerkat Marketing and VP of Marketing at seoplus+, tells David DiNardo how he went from co-founding Faces Magazine with his brother in 2007 to running marketing at Welch LLP and Keynote Search, building Meerkat as a side business, and selling it to seoplus+ in August 2025. He lays out what actually works in marketing now that AI has flooded every channel with generic content: authenticity, founder-led content, LinkedIn for B2B, and paid ads for lead generation.

02 KEY TAKEAWAYS
  1. Kyle co-founded Faces Magazine in Halifax in 2007, franchised it to six cities, shrank back to two, and sold both to advertisers he already knew after seven years.
  2. He ran Meerkat Marketing as a side business for three to four years, mostly on referrals from friends, before going full-time in 2023; seoplus+ acquired it in August 2025 after years of mutual referrals built trust.
  3. For a personal brand on LinkedIn, two posts a month is his minimum and roughly one a week is typical; for B2B brands the sweet spot is one to two posts a week, and more than that annoys the LinkedIn audience.
  4. Paid ads are for lead generation and organic social is for warming people up, so a Meta ad can bring the lead in while organic content makes the close easy.
  5. SEO is a long game: Kyle says it took about two years to get Keynote Search to number one for its terms, but the ranking has held for two to three years since.
  6. His rule of thumb on budget: studies say 10% of revenue for high growth, but most Meerkat clients spend around 5% and some large companies as little as 2 to 3%.
  7. AI-generated strategy and content is always somewhat generic, so the brands winning now are the ones that lean back into creativity and authenticity; on Instagram, overly polished content now gets skipped as an ad.
