01 SHOW NOTES

He spent 20 years as a business lawyer, went to Australia for a client who backed out, and kept the idea for himself. Ten years later he opened Kids Can Swim in Kanata — six weeks before COVID. They closed and reopened eight times. Today it&#39;s three Ottawa locations, 175 team members, thousands of kids a week, and a national expansion plan.

In this conversation: why the pools are shallow and heated to 32°C, the "active learning time" metric that took kids from 4 minutes of practice to 18, how a free trial converts at 67%, the real numbers behind a $3M build-out, EBITDA multiples by location count, and the mentor who asked him "are you going to do it, or just shut up?"
